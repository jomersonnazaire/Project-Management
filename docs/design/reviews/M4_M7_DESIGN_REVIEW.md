Findings:
- DR-24 (Medium): while a timer runs, the top-bar pill (task name, time and Time out) doesn't shrink at 390, so every page is 544–738px wide and scrolls sideways. On My tasks the page shrinks to fit 615px (see `m47/admin-my-tasks-390.png`). Below 576px, cut the pill down to a dot, the elapsed time and an icon-only Time out, and drop the task name. Give the navbar `min-width:0` and let "+ Quick activity" / "Record leave" collapse to icons.
- DR-25 (Medium): hours from timed entries show as raw decimals: Time logging "0.03333333333333333" (row and Total), and Reports › Effort variance Actual "4.883333333333333" / "0.3666666666666667". Format them when shown and in the CSV: HH:MM for timed time (doc 14 §12.2), or at most 2 decimals where the column is in hours. Use the same format in "Logged this week".
- DR-26 (Medium): the DAR preview doesn't match the sample layout (FR-DAR-07/08, mockup v0.8.7). It has no navy #00467f header block (white title and "Generated range"): the title is plain heading text on white, and the PDF also starts with black text. At 1440 the 11-column table scrolls sideways and Activity Remarks is cut off ("ACTIVIT…"). Column headers are upper-cased ("TIME IN") while the sample uses "Time In". Add the navy band with white title and range to the preview and PDF. Fit all 11 columns at ≥1200px (smaller report font, wrap Remarks, fixed table layout), and set `text-transform:none` on the report headers.
- DR-27 (Medium): Dashboard › My projects has one "Health" column that shows status words for most rows (COMPLETED, ACTIVE, PLANNING; only one row says AT RISK). There's no Status column and no Waiting on client column, both of which FR-PMV-01 and the mockup include. Show a health badge (On track / At risk / Delayed / On hold) and add a separate Status column. Also add a "Waiting on client" count that links to the items.
- DR-28 (Low): on a 390 phone with no timer running, the Reports tabs (686px) and the DAR action row push the page sideways. The DAR row is 555px wide and Export PDF / Export Excel sit outside the card. Make the tabs a horizontal scroller (`flex-nowrap; overflow-x:auto`) and stack the DAR buttons full width (or put Export in a menu).
- DR-29 (Low): Team & workload as a Member calls GET /api/v1/teams and gets a 403 on every visit, which leaves the "All teams" filter empty. Take the team list from the workload response (or allow reading your own teams), and hide the filter when there's only one team.
- DR-30 (Low): the first Time in of the day asks "Where are you working today?" and also shows a second "Location: The day's location · Change for this entry only" select in the same modal. Hide the per-entry Location on the entry that sets the day's location. It's only needed once a day location exists.
- DR-31 (Low): the running task row on Today shows "–" in the TODAY column with no row tint, where the mockup shows "01:12 RUNNING" on a green row. The top-bar pill shows HH:MM, so it reads "00:00" for the first minute and looks frozen (the mockup shows 01:12:07). Show the live elapsed time in the row with the running tint, and either show seconds in the pill or add a pulsing dot.
- DR-32 (Low): Today first renders without the TODAY/Time in column, the day chip and "Hours rendered", and they appear about 3–5 s later, which shifts the table. On the first visit the member's Today had no Time in buttons at all for several seconds. Draw the columns straight away with skeleton cells or disabled Time in buttons until tracker data arrives.
- DR-33 (Low): Half day AM and Half day PM of the same type on the same date show as two rows in My leave and Team on leave (Maria, Oct 13 Vacation AM + PM). FR-LV-11 says to show them as one full day. Merge them into one row ("Full day (AM + PM)", 1 working day) and keep per-half cancel in that row's menu.
- DR-34 (Low): Team & workload shows raw role codes under names (PROJECT_MANAGER, CONSULTANT, QA_TESTER). Show the Job role label ("Project Manager", "Consultant") like Admin › Users.
- DR-35 (Low): a quick activity or manual entry can't be deleted anywhere. The day timesheet ✎ modal has only Save, and Time logging lists project entries only. Its Delete uses the browser's native `confirm()` ("Delete this time entry?"), unlike the app's other confirm modals. Add "Delete entry" to the edit modal while the day isn't submitted or locked, and use the app modal for every delete confirm.
- DR-36 (Low): the sidebar "SETUP" section label is #a7acb2 on white, 2.29:1, which fails AA. Use secondary #5d6878 (5.65:1).
- DR-37 (Cosmetic): CSS capitalizes tab labels: "Assigned To Me", "I'm Accountable", "To Review", "Day Timesheet", "Saved Reports", "My Leave", "Team On Leave", "Activity Types", "Effort Variance". The mockup and doc 14 use sentence case, so remove `text-transform:capitalize` from the tabs.
- DR-38 (Cosmetic):
  - The Add entry modal shows ISO dates ("Add entry · 2026-10-09", "Philippine time · 2026-10-09"). Use "Fri, Oct 9".
  - The sidebar truncates "Daily Accomplishment…". Let it wrap to 2 lines as in the mockup.
  - Time in / Time out are outline buttons, where the mockup has a green "▶ Time in" and a red "■ Time out".
  - Team balances show "-3.5" with a hyphen and no Negative badge. Use "−3.5 Negative" as in My balances.
  - The "Hours rendered" note says "Sum of time in/out pairs" but the total includes Hours-only entries. Use "Sum of the day's entries (exact minutes)".
